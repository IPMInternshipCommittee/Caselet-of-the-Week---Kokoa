Kokoa — The India Play

This caselet works through an artisan chocolate brand's pan-India expansion decision using a simple four-step framework: understand the opportunity, size the market, assess the expansion risks, and choose the right channels.

Not every number deserves equal weight. Focus first on separating an upper-bound estimate from the realistic addressable market.

Understand the Opportunity → Size the Market → Assess the Risks → Choose the Channels
  • Is the market large enough? Establish whether the addressable premium chocolate market can support Kokoa's revenue ambition.
  • What are the major risks of expansion? Identify what could undermine the brand premium Kokoa is trying to preserve.
  • Which channels should Kokoa use? Compare channels on speed to scale, margin contribution and brand fit, and sequence them over time.

Market SizingEstimating the potential opportunity for a product or service using a structured set of assumptions.
Market ShareThe percentage of the relevant market captured by a company. Comparing a revenue target with market size helps determine the share required to achieve that target.
Market Entry StrategyDeciding how a company should enter or expand into a market, including the channels, timing and level of investment required.
Channel StrategyChoosing and sequencing sales or distribution channels based on factors such as speed, margins, customer fit and brand considerations.
Phased ExpansionEntering a market progressively, validating demand and building capabilities before committing to larger or harder-to-reverse investments.
